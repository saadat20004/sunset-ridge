The land

Remote feeling, right above town.

Sunset Ridge sits on a forested hillside with nearly 800 feet of elevation to explore. Hang a hammock in the shade, wander the trails on foot or by bike, and find a rock to sit on while the valley turns gold.

Guests keep saying the same thing: it feels completely tucked away, yet Bellingham's food, lakes and famous mountain bike trails are only a short drive down the hill.

Good to know

Before you arrive

Quick answers to the questions guests ask most. Tap a question to open it.

Still have a question? Add it to your booking request and Shaums will reply within the hour.

  • Alderwood and Cottonwood Cabin need an AWD or 4x4 vehicle (or you can hike in to the cabin). Dogwood and Cedarwood recommend one. Avoid low clearance cars, the access tracks are bumpy.

  • Geneva, Whatcom County, about 15 minutes from downtown Bellingham. The exact address and directions are shared once your booking is confirmed.

  • Every site has a toilet nearby and potable water. Showers are available at the Sunset Ridge Trailer only.

  • Very peaceful during the day. Light sleepers may notice a faint hum from the highway late at night, so pack earplugs just in case.

  • Paddleboard on Lake Whatcom or Toad Lake, ride Bellingham's mountain bike trails, explore the food trucks in town, or day trip to North Cascades National Park.
